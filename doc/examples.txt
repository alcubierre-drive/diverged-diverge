Namespace: Examples
Examples for divERGe::
Examples are found in the <examples/ at
https://git.rwth-aachen.de/frg/diverge/-/blob/master/examples/> directory. In
the git repository, they are furthermore grouped into programming languages. In
the release tarball, all examples are merged within examples/.

For step-by-step tutorials of the python interface, look at
<examples/*-?-*.py at
https://git.rwth-aachen.de/frg/diverge/-/blob/master/examples/python_tutorial/>,
i.e., either the files
