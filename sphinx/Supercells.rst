Supercells
==========

.. caution:: The default sign convention of supercell mappings is *not* in line
   with divERGe's standard sign convention. Use the function
   :c:func:`diverge_supercell_set_sign` with parameter ``-1.0`` to take care of
   this before calling any of the supercell routines.
